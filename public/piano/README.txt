These are the velocity-8 MP3s as packaged in @audio-samples/piano-mp3-velocity8 1.0.5 (MIT,
https://github.com/darosh/samples-piano-mp3), one sample every three semitones from A0 to C8, renamed without
"#" (D#4 is Ds4) so the names work in URLs. The app's piano sound (src/audio/piano.ts) plays them.
